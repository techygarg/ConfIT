How to work: run the checks only through the `verifier-haiku` agent, never with make or dotnet yourself. If they fail, diagnose and fix it yourself: use the failure hint in the verdict to search the failing log rather than reading it whole, make the fix, then run `verifier-haiku` again to confirm. Repeat the fix and confirm steps at most twice. Finish with the final status, what failed, the cause, and what you changed.
